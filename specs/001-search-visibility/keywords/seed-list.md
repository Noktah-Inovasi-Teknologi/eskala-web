# Keyword seed list (T035, FR-009, FR-010)

**Sources (2026-10-16)**: Google Indonesia autocomplete (`hl=id`, `gl=id`) for 51 seed phrases, plus phrases from the site's own copy and the [test prompts](test-prompts.md). **✓** means Google suggested the phrase itself, so people search it. Blank means a phrase we expect but Google didn't suggest: Keyword Planner will tell (O-8).

**What the autocomplete already shows**
- **Searches for a service provider are rare but real**: "jasa digital marketing klinik", "jasa marketing klinik", "digital marketing klinik gigi / kecantikan". Almost nobody competes for them (see the ledger's competitor scan), so they're the first pages to win.
- **Questions get far more suggestions**: "strategi pemasaran rumah sakit", "cara promosi klinik …", "konten edukasi kesehatan …", "permenkes iklan dan publikasi pelayanan kesehatan". These are FAQ answers now and article topics later. They're also where chatbots get their answers.
- **Job-search phrases** ("loker", "gaji", "jobdesk", "kerjanya apa") and **specific hospital names** came up often. They're left out: those searchers aren't buying.
- **Generic agency terms** ("creative agency", "healthcare marketing agency") came back empty or foreign. FR-010 doesn't target them. "creative agency klinik / kesehatan" stays on the list only so Keyword Planner can settle FR-012.

## How to run it in Keyword Planner (O-8)

Paste the **Phrase** column (all 62) into *Get search volume and forecasts*. Location **Indonesia**, language **Indonesian**, last 12 months. Download the CSV to `keywords/planner-export.csv`.
