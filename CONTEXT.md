# Eskala

Eskala's public website and the services it sells to klinik pratama dan utama, rumah sakit, and dokter umum maupun spesialis across Indonesia.

## Language

### Brand

**Partner digital marketing dan komunikasi kesehatan**:
How Eskala describes itself: a partner that works alongside a facility's in-house marketing team on digital marketing and health communication.
_Avoid_: creative agency, tim kreatif kesehatan (in visible copy; "creative agency" may appear only as a search term in metadata and one FAQ answer when keyword data shows demand)

### Search visibility

**Target keyword**:
A healthcare-qualified search phrase Eskala wants to rank for, mapped to the one page that answers it.
_Avoid_: generic agency terms (digital marketing agency, creative agency) as targets

**Test prompt**:
A question a prospect might ask a chatbot, used to check whether the answer names Eskala.
_Avoid_: query, keyword (those are for search engines)

**Source tag**:
The short ending of a pre-filled WhatsApp message naming where the visit started and the page the visitor was on.
_Avoid_: tracking code, UTM

**Consent choice**:
A visitor's answer to whether Eskala may use analytics on the site, changeable at any time.
_Avoid_: cookie acceptance
